Photography Guidelines for Accurate Diagnosis
  • Focus on a single leaf

    Capture the leaf clearly filling the frame rather than the entire tomato plant or field.

  • Natural, bright lighting

    Photograph outdoors under daylight. Avoid heavy shadows, intense backlighting, or direct flash reflections.

  • Capture visible lesions

    Ensure spots, yellowing margins, or blight marks are sharp and in focus.

  • Avoid blurry or distant captures

    Images taken too far away or out-of-focus lower AI model classification confidence.
